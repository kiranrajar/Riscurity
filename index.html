CASE WALKTHROUGH

Real Audit Finding: Unblocking a $280k Enterprise Deal

A real walkthrough of how our offensive testing uncovered a deal-breaking vulnerability and delivered an audit-ready resolution in 4 business days.

Client Profile: Series A B2B Fintech SaaS (22 FTEs)
Target Environment: AWS ECS · Next.js / Node.js API · PostgreSQL
Engagement Goal: Pre-Enterprise Customer Vendor Assessment
01 · SCENARIO

Mid-Sales Enterprise Block

The client was finalizing a $280,000/year contract with a regional banking partner. The enterprise buyer required third-party penetration test results and an executive attestation letter within 10 days before signature.

02 · CRITICAL FINDING

Cross-Tenant Broken Object Authorization (BOLA/IDOR)

During authenticated API testing, our engineers discovered an IDOR vulnerability in the /api/v2/analytics/reports/{id} endpoint. By tampering with the report UUID parameter, a user in Organization A could query confidential financial telemetry from Organization B without authorization.

03 · ENGINEERING FIX

Collaborative 48-Hour Patch & Test Suite

Instead of sending a detached PDF, our engineers hopped on Slack with their backend team, authored scoped tenant-isolation middleware, and added automated integration tests to their CI pipeline to prevent future regressions.

04 · BUSINESS OUTCOME

Contract Signed & Clean Attestation Issued

We re-tested the environment within 24 hours of patch deployment, confirmed zero vulnerability residue, and issued an official Letter of Attestation. The bank approved the vendor security review with zero objections and signed the contract.
